Reference sign videos in this folder
====================================

The 22 files <CODE>.mp4 are reference videos from the SWL-LSE dataset
(SignaMed Word-Level LSE), Universidade de Vigo.

Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
         https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Source:  Zenodo record 13691887, file VIDEOS_REF.zip
         https://zenodo.org/records/13691887
Paper:   "SWL-LSE: A Dataset of Spanish Sign Language Health Signs with an
         ISLR Baseline Method", Technologies 2024, 12(10), 205.
         DOI 10.3390/technologies12100205

Changes: 22 of the 300 reference videos were selected and renamed from their
record id to the sign label given in the dataset's annotations. The video
data itself is unchanged (H.264 in MP4, 480x480, 20 frames per second, no
audio).

The signs are Spanish Sign Language (LSE), not Tunisian Sign Language.
